# HR-Analytics-Streamlit-App
Key Features
✅ Employee Count & Attrition Tracking
✅ Attrition Analysis by Salary, Gender & Education
✅ Job Role-wise Attrition Analysis
✅ Employee Tenure (Years at Company) Analysis
✅ Performance Rating & Job Satisfaction Comparison
✅ Interactive and User-Friendly Dashboard

💡 Major Insights
📌 Overall Attrition Rate: 16.08% among 1,470 employees
📌 Employees with lower salaries showed higher attrition
📌 Most employees left within their first few years at the company
📌 Sales Executives, Research Scientists, and Laboratory Technicians had the highest attrition counts
📌 Employee satisfaction levels played an important role in retention trends
